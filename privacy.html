Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy covers Calder (a home screen for smart displays such as Meta Portal) and its companion Android app Calder Pocket. Both run entirely on your own devices. There is no Calder server or account: we do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your data.

Google user data we access

YouTube (YouTube API Services)

When you sign in to Music on a Portal with Google's device sign-in, Calder asks for read-only access to your YouTube account (scope youtube.readonly) and uses it only to:

  • show your channel name and picture on the Portal so you know who is signed in;
  • list your playlists and their videos, your liked videos and the channels you subscribe to;
  • search YouTube for music when you ask it to.

Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player. Calder cannot post, like, comment, upload, or change anything in your account.

Google Photos (Calder Pocket)

Calder Pocket uses the Google Photos Picker (scope photospicker.mediaitems.readonly) so you can choose photos; only the photos you pick are downloaded and sent to your own Portal's photo frame on your home network.

How the data is stored and used

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the device you signed in on, encrypted with that device's Android Keystore, and are excluded from backups.
  • Library information (playlist names, video titles and thumbnails) is cached on the device to keep the screen responsive and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • Your data is used only to provide the features you see on your device. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or training AI models, it is never sold, and no person reads it.
  • Data goes only between your devices and Google's servers. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Limited Use

Calder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Calder uses YouTube API Services. By using Calder's Music you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: signing out and deleting data

  • On the Portal, open Music → your account → Sign out. This revokes Calder's access with Google and deletes the tokens and cached library data from that Portal.
  • You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions (or via Google security settings).
  • Uninstalling Calder or Calder Pocket deletes all of its data from that device.

Children

Calder is a household device app and is not directed at children; signing in requires a Google Account that is allowed to use YouTube.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if anything changes. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
